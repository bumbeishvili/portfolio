I ran a D3.js workshop at DataFest Tbilisi 2022, held over November 17–19 at Rooms Hotel.

Small room, everyone with a laptop open, working through [Observable](https://observablehq.com/@bumbeishvili/d3-workshop-cheatsheet) notebooks — SVG first, then data, scales and arcs.

I put the materials together a few days before and they've held up since. I ran the same workshop again a year later [at Kutaisi International University](/blog/kiu-d3-workshop).
