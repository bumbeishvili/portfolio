We came third at the MCP/AI Hackathon Tbilisi, held on May 4 2025 at D Block Workspace in Stamba.

Eight hours — coding from 11:30, submissions closed at 17:30, demos and judging after. Teams capped at three, 128 registered, and projects with Georgian relevance were given preference.

Run by Four Loop Pod, Mazeg and Tbilisi AI Meetups. Judges were Raphael Kalandadze (Wandero AI, AI-Lab Tbilisi), Giorgi Jvaridze (MaxinAI, Olmait) and Giorgi Jibladze (Omedia).
