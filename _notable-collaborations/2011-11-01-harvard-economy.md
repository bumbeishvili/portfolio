The Economy in Place is a Harvard Kennedy School initiative with interactive dashboards for 700+ U.S. commuting zones.

It covers economic conditions, policy flows, and energy-transition signals to support place-based analysis.

<br/>
Live website: [economyinplace.com](https://economyinplace.com/).
